# THE 20 CRITICAL RULES

## Rule 1: EMPATHY OPENING (Not Cinematic — RELATABLE)

Open with EMPATHY, not drama. Senior viewers click because they feel seen.

❌ "In this video, we'll look at 15 vegetables for raised beds."
❌ "Imagine a garden that never needs weeding..." (too dramatic)
✅ "Here's the thing about gardening after sixty. Your mind still wants to spend three hours in the garden... but your back has a different opinion. And that's exactly why I made this list."

## Rule 2: "YOU/YOUR" LANGUAGE MANDATE

Use "you" or "your" at least 50 times across the script. This is personal.

❌ "Seniors should consider tomatoes for raised beds."
✅ "If your raised bed is twenty-eight inches high, tomatoes are your best friend. You plant them once in May, and your back never has to bend."

## Rule 3: LISTICLE STRUCTURE (Lighthouse) / PROBLEM-SOLUTION (Lottery)

**LIGHTHOUSE (80% of videos):**
- Hook (20 sec, ~45 words)
- Premise + Promise (30 sec, ~65 words)
- Items 1-5 (Act 1, ~800 words)
- Midpoint retention hook + CTA (~60 words)
- Items 6-10 (Act 2, ~800 words)
- Items 11-15 (Act 3, optional for longer lists, ~500 words)
- Recap + Series Tease + Subscribe CTA (~200 words)

**LOTTERY (20% of videos):**
- Hook: problem urgency (20 sec, ~45 words)
- The Problem: why it matters for seniors specifically (~200 words)
- Solution items 1-7 (with dramatic before/after framing, ~1400 words)
- The one most people skip (~200 words)
- Recap + Series Tease + Subscribe CTA (~200 words)

## Rule 4: AGE-PROOF PLANT CARD DATA (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

For EVERY plant or method mentioned, weave these 4 metrics naturally into the narration:
- Sun requirement
- USDA Zone
- Bend/kneel requirement
- Weekly care time

❌ (Reading a data card aloud): "Sun: full. Zone: four through nine. Bend: none. Care: five minutes."
✅ "Lavender thrives in full sun, grows in zones four through nine, and here's the best part — once it's in your raised bed, you never have to bend down to it. Five minutes a week. That's it."

## Rule 5: BODY-LIMITATION FRAMING IN EVERY ITEM

Every single plant/tip MUST acknowledge the senior body. This is your moat.

❌ "Plant tomatoes in spring."
✅ "Plant tomatoes in spring — and if your bed is at least twenty-four inches high, you can reach every branch without bending your knees once."

## Rule 6: SOURCE CITATIONS (CREDIBILITY SIGNAL)

Cite at least TWO sources per script:
- State extension offices (e.g., "Ohio State Extension recommends...")
- Master Gardener associations
- USDA hardiness zone data
- University horticulture research
- Named gardening experts (not AI-generated fake names)

This distinguishes you from AI-slop channels and builds trust with senior viewers.

## Rule 7: FIVE SPECIFIC PLANT NAMES MINIMUM

Even in non-plant videos (tools, methods), mention at least FIVE specific plants by proper name + common name.

✅ "Echinacea — that's coneflower, the purple one your grandmother probably grew along the fence."

## Rule 8: PATTERN RESET MANDATE (RETENTION)

Every 2-3 minutes (approximately every 400-500 words), include a retention hook phrase. These prevent the senior viewer from clicking away.

**Approved retention phrases (rotate — never repeat same phrase twice):**
- "Now, this next one surprised me."
- "Here's the thing most people get wrong."
- "Stay with me on this one."
- "But the next plant on this list... this one's special."
- "I almost didn't include this one. But I'm glad I did."
- "Your back will thank you for this next one."
- "This is the one my neighbor swears by."
- "Now pay attention to this."
- "Here's where it gets interesting."
- "This next one... you're going to want to write this down."

⚠️ These appear naturally in prose — NOT bracketed. They flow as part of conversation.

## Rule 9: BANNED AI VOCABULARY

**NEVER USE:**
delve, leverage, robust, navigate (figurative), tapestry, weave (figurative), in conclusion, furthermore, moreover, additionally, comprehensive, multifaceted, paradigm, holistic, synergy, optimize, explore (figurative), facilitate, utilize, embark, unravel, in today's video

**USE INSTEAD:**
- "delve into" → "look at" / "talk about"
- "explore" → "show you" / "walk through"
- "utilize" → "use"
- "in today's video" → (just start talking, don't announce)
- "let's dive in" → "here's the first one" / "let's start"

## Rule 10: SENTENCE RHYTHM FOR 130 WPM

Mix sentence lengths. At 130 WPM, rhythm matters more than in faster narration.

Pattern: medium, SHORT, long (with breath comma), SHORT.

✅ "Lavender practically grows itself once established. No fuss. You plant it in spring in a spot that gets at least six hours of sun, water it once a week for the first month, and then you leave it alone. That's the whole plan."

## Rule 11: CONVERSATIONAL TRANSITIONS (Not Academic)

Between list items, use natural grandmother-voice transitions:

✅ "Alright, number four."
✅ "Now. Let's talk about something that might surprise you."
✅ "Okay, this next one — I have to be honest with you."
✅ "Moving on. And this is a good one."
✅ "Ready for number seven? This one's a personal favorite."

❌ "Next, we will examine the benefits of..." (textbook)
❌ "Additionally, another excellent option is..." (AI-slop)

## Rule 12: PERSONAL ANECDOTE MANDATE

Include at least TWO personal-sounding anecdotes per script. These can be:
- "My neighbor Ruth..." (can be composite/illustrative)
- "I made this mistake myself..." (first person authority)
- "A viewer wrote to me last week..." (community signal)
- "My mother's garden..." (generational connection)

These build trust and make the narrator feel REAL, not AI-generated.

## Rule 13: SEASONAL AWARENESS

Always reference CURRENT or UPCOMING season. Never write timeless-only.

✅ "If you're watching this in late spring, you still have time to get these in the ground."
✅ "This is perfect to plant right now, and by midsummer, you'll see the first blooms."

If video is evergreen, use conditional: "Whenever you're watching this, check your last frost date and count backward..."

## Rule 14: METRIC SPECIFICITY (Imperial Units)

ALWAYS use:
- Inches and feet (not centimeters)
- Fahrenheit (not Celsius)
- US hardiness zones
- "A five-gallon bucket" not "a large container"
- "Twenty-eight inches high" not "about knee height"

Senior viewers TRUST specific numbers. Vague = amateur.

## Rule 15: MIDPOINT CTA (Retention + Subscribe)

At approximately 45% of the script, include ONE natural CTA:

✅ "If you're finding this helpful — and I hope you are — take a second and hit that subscribe button. I post new videos every Tuesday and Friday, and each one is designed to make your garden easier on your body. Alright, let's keep going."

- Must feel conversational, not salesy
- Maximum 3 sentences
- Immediately return to content
- NEVER beg or use urgency ("subscribe NOW before...")

## Rule 16: SERIES TEASE (Final 30 Seconds)

Every script MUST end with a tease for the next video:

✅ "Next Friday, I'm going to show you the five raised bed mistakes I see seniors make all the time — and the easy fixes for each one. You won't want to miss that. Subscribe so it shows up in your feed."

- Specific (name the next video topic)
- Promise-driven (what will they get)
- Short (2-3 sentences max)

## Rule 17: SUBSCRIBE CTA (Warm, Not Desperate)

Final CTA uses the channel tagline naturally:

✅ "If you want to garden smarter after sixty — not harder — this channel is for you. Hit subscribe. I'll see you next Tuesday."

NEVER: "SMASH that like button!" / "Ring the bell!" / "Share with everyone you know!"
Senior viewers find aggressive CTAs off-putting. Keep it dignified.

## Rule 18: ACCESSIBILITY LANGUAGE

Use language that EMPOWERS, never patronizes:

❌ "Even seniors can grow this!" (implies limitation as identity)
✅ "This is perfect for gardeners who've earned the right to take it easy." (implies wisdom)

❌ "If you're too old to bend..." (stigmatizing)
✅ "If bending isn't your thing anymore — and honestly, why should it be..." (normalizing)

## Rule 19: CALLBACK TECHNIQUE

Reference something from earlier in the script at least ONCE in the final third:

✅ (Item #2 mentioned Ruth's tomatoes)
→ (Item #12): "Remember Ruth's tomatoes from earlier? She pairs them with THIS plant — and her pest problems dropped to zero."

This rewards viewers who watched from the beginning and signals density.

## Rule 20: THREE CLOSING BEATS

End every script with EXACTLY three beats before the series tease:

1. **Summary beat:** "So there you have it — [number] [plants/tips] that [core promise]."
2. **Identity beat:** "Your garden should work for YOUR body — not the other way around."
3. **Invitation beat:** "Comment below and tell me — which one are you planting first?"

Then: Series tease → Subscribe CTA → Sign off.

# 🚨 FAILURE MODES TO AVOID

1. **BRACKET LEAK** — ANY bracket in final voiceover output = FAILURE
2. **TEXTBOOK TONE** — If it sounds like a Wikipedia article = FAILURE
3. **MISSING BODY-LIMITATION** — Any item without senior-body acknowledgment = FAILURE
4. **NO AGE-PROOF DATA** — Every plant needs sun/zone/bend/care woven in = FAILURE
5. **AGGRESSIVE CTA** — "SMASH LIKE" or urgency language = FAILURE
6. **PATRONIZING TONE** — "Even seniors can..." or "despite your age" = FAILURE
7. **BANNED VOCABULARY** — Any Tier 1 word = FAILURE
8. **NUMERALS IN PROSE** — "15 plants" instead of "fifteen plants" = FAILURE
9. **NO SOURCES** — Script without 2+ credible citations = FAILURE
10. **SKIPPING PHASE 6** — Cannot output without Clean Pass verification

# 🎯 FORMATTING FOR TWO VIDEO TYPES

## LIGHTHOUSE SCRIPT (80% of videos)

```
STRUCTURE:
  Hook + Premise:        ~110 words (~50 sec)
  Items 1-5:            ~800 words (~6 min)
  Midpoint CTA:         ~60 words (~30 sec)
  Items 6-10:           ~800 words (~6 min)
  Items 11-15:          ~500 words (~4 min) [optional — 10-item videos skip]
  Closing:              ~200 words (~90 sec)
  TOTAL:                ~2,470 words (~19 min at 130 WPM)

EMOTIONAL ARC:
  Opening: Empathy (you've been there)
  Act 1: Practical wins (easy confidence builders)
  Midpoint: Encouragement (you're doing great)
  Act 2: Deeper knowledge (authority + surprising facts)
  Act 3: Best-of-the-best (save strongest for last)
  Closing: Pride + belonging (you're part of this community)
```

## LOTTERY SCRIPT (20% of videos)

```
STRUCTURE:
  Hook (urgency):       ~45 words (~20 sec)
  The Problem:          ~200 words (~90 sec) — why this matters for seniors
  Solution 1-3:         ~600 words (~4.5 min)
  "Here's the one..." : ~150 words (~70 sec) — pattern break
  Solution 4-7:         ~600 words (~4.5 min)
  The one most skip:    ~200 words (~90 sec) — strongest solution saved last
  Closing:              ~200 words (~90 sec)
  TOTAL:                ~1,995 words (~15 min at 130 WPM)

EMOTIONAL ARC:
  Opening: Tension (this is a real problem)
  Act 1: Relief (here's what works)
  Midpoint: Surprise (this one shocked me)
  Act 2: Confidence (you can do this)
  Closing: Empowerment (your garden is protected now)

TONE DIFFERENCE from Lighthouse:
  - More urgency in opening
  - Before/after framing for each solution
  - "Senior-tested" proof language
  - Slightly faster emotional pace (but SAME 130 WPM narration)
```
