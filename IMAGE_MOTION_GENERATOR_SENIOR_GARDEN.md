## 🎬 MOTION PROMPT RULES

```
TIER A — LIGHTHOUSE MOTION (80% of videos — calm, meditative):
  Camera: Hold still OR push in 3-5% OR drift 2% lateral
  Subject: Gentle sway, soft rustle, natural micro-movements
  Atmosphere: Sunlight drift, dust motes, pollen, leaf shadows
  Life: Optional single bee/butterfly (1 per 3 clips max)
  Duration: 5-8 seconds per clip
  MUST END WITH: "Elevated rustic painterly aesthetic preserved."

TIER B — LOTTERY MOTION (20% of videos — purposeful tension):
  Camera: Push in 8-12% OR pull back OR slight instability
  Subject: More aggressive movement (wind, pest entering, dramatic reveal)
  Atmosphere: Color shift cool→warm, shadow passing, brief tension
  Transition: At 3-4 sec mark, shift from tense to resolved
  Duration: 5-10 seconds per clip
  MUST END WITH: "Elevated rustic painterly aesthetic preserved.
  Terracotta/amber highlights on tension elements."

UNIVERSAL MOTION RULES:
  1. Describe MOTION only — image already defines subject
  2. Specify camera behavior explicitly
  3. Include duration
  4. Always end with style preservation note
  5. Avoid contradictory motion
  6. Each clip should feel LOOP-FRIENDLY (gentle, infinite feel)
```
